# Sales Quick Reference — Print This

> *One page. Keep in front of you while calling.*

---

## 90-Second Cold Call

> *"Hi [name], this is [your name]. Quick question — when someone searches '[their service] in [city]' on Google, does your business show up on the first page?"*

**If no/unsure:**
> *"That's why I'm calling. I help local businesses get to the top of Google — website, Google profile management, weekly content that brings in customers. I'm taking a few new clients this month. If I can get you on page one within 60 days, worth 10 minutes to talk?"*

**Yes →** "Great, I'll text you my calendar link right now."
**No →** "No problem. Want me to send you a free report of where you rank right now?"

## Objections

| They say | You say |
|----------|---------|
| "No time" | "Exactly why I'm calling — zero work for you" |
| "Already rank" | "You're #[X], competitor [name] is above you. I fix that." |
| "Too expensive" | "One extra customer pays for this 10x. What's a customer worth to you?" |
| "I'll think about it" | "I'll send your free ranking report. Look it over, call me if you see opportunity." |
| "I can do it myself" | "You could. But every client I have tried and stopped after 2 weeks. Consistency is what gets results." |

## 3 Steps to a Yes

1. **Find the pain** — "Do you show up on Google?"
2. **Show the gap** — "Your competitor [name] ranks above you and they have a website"
3. **Offer the fix** — "I handle everything for $197/mo. You don't do anything."

---

## What to Say When They Ask "How Does It Work?"

> *"I take over your Google Business Profile — optimize it, post photos of your work every week, get your customers to leave reviews. Google sees activity and pushes you higher. Within 60 days you'll show up for more searches and get more calls. All you do is send me a few photos of your work each week — or I take them when I visit."*

---

## After the Call — Always Do This

- ✅ **Sent a text** with your name and calendar link
- ✅ **Sent free ranking report** (if they said no)
- ✅ **Logged the result** in your lead tracker
- ✅ **Follow up in 7 days** if they said "think about it"
