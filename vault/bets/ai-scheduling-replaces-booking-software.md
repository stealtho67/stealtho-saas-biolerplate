# AI Scheduling Replaces Traditional Booking Software

> **Hypothesis:** Within 12 months, barbershops will prefer AI-driven scheduling (auto-fill cancellations, smart reminders, dynamic pricing) over traditional calendar booking apps.
>
> **Entry date:** 2026-06-01

## The Bet

The next wave of barber booking won't be about "who has the best calendar UI" — it'll be about **who maximizes the barber's revenue per hour using AI**. Booksy and the incumbents are calendar apps. The winner will be an AI scheduling layer.

## Signal

- Booksy's stock is down — barbers are actively seeking alternatives
- Gemini/Claude API costs are dropping fast enough to make AI scheduling free-tier viable
- Barbers already use AI tools (ChatGPT for content, Meta AI for ads) — they're not afraid of it

## Expected Outcome

- NextCut can leapfrog incumbents by launching AI-assisted scheduling as a v2 differentiator
- If we don't build it, someone else will — the window is ~12 months
